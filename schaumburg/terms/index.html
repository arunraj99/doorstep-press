Terms and Refund Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The short version: you pay to hold your category. We design your ad, you approve it, and we mail the card to nearly 5,000 homes in Schaumburg 60193. If fewer than ten spots sell, the card does not mail and you get a full refund. Questions? Call or text Arun at (610) 569-8530.

1. Who we are

Doorstep Press publishes The Schaumburg Spotlight. Doorstep Press is a brand of Stellogics Inc. Charges appear on your card statement as STELLOGICS.

2. What you are buying

You are buying advertising space on The Schaumburg Spotlight, a 9x12 inch full color card mailed through USPS Every Door Direct Mail to the homes on the carrier routes we select in Schaumburg, ZIP 60193. Each card carries up to 16 ad spots on two sides. Every spot is the same size and the same price. We do not sell premium positions, and we decide where each ad is placed.

3. Spots and pricing

  • 1 spot: $500 for a single issue, or $450 per issue on the six month plan.
  • 2 spots, shown side by side as one double size ad: $900 for a single issue, or $800 per issue on the six month plan.
  • Each business can buy a maximum of 2 spots per issue.

Prices are in US dollars. We do not currently charge sales tax on advertising.

4. Payment holds your category

Your category is held only once payment is complete. Categories are first come, first paid. If two businesses in the same category try to buy, the first completed payment gets the spot and we fully refund the other.

5. Category exclusivity

For each issue, only one business per category appears on the card. We define categories based on a business's main service (for example: roofing, HVAC, dentist, plumbing). If businesses offer overlapping services, we decide the category in good faith and will tell you before you pay if there could be an overlap. Exclusivity applies to the card itself and does not limit other advertising outside The Schaumburg Spotlight.

6. The full refund guarantee

If fewer than ten spots sell for an issue, that issue does not mail and every advertiser in that issue receives a full refund within 7 days of the space deadline. On the six month plan, you are not charged for any issue that does not mail.

7. Cancelling and other refunds

Single issue: you can cancel for a full refund within 3 days of paying, as long as we have not started designing your ad. After design starts, the payment is non refundable, except under the guarantee in section 6 or an error by us under section 11.

Six month plan: you are billed once a month, one issue at a time, for six issues. You can cancel at any time before your next monthly charge by calling, texting or emailing us. Cancelling stops future charges and ends your category lock. Issues already charged follow the single issue rules above.

To cancel or request a refund, contact us. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

8. Your ad: content, design and approval

  • Ad design is included. You send us your logo, photos, offer and contact details. If you do not have artwork, we can work from your website.
  • You get a proof before anything prints, with up to two rounds of changes included.
  • Nothing prints without your approval. If we do not receive your materials or approval by the deadline we give you, we may run a clean version of your ad using your business name and contact details, or move your spot to the next issue, whichever you prefer.
  • You confirm you have the right to use any logos, photos and text you send us, and that your ad and offer are truthful and lawful.
  • We may decline or edit ads that are misleading, unlawful, offensive or inappropriate for a community mailer. If we decline your ad, you get a full refund.
  • You are responsible for honoring any offer, discount or promotion in your ad.

9. Mailing and delivery

We mail through USPS Every Door Direct Mail. Household counts are based on USPS route data at the time of mailing and may vary slightly. Mailing dates we share are our planned dates. Once the card is handed to USPS, delivery timing is up to USPS, and we are not responsible for postal delays. The week the card mails, you receive the USPS receipt, the route map and a printed copy of the card.

10. Tracking and results

Each ad gets its own QR code and offer code, and you receive a report of scans after each issue. Advertising results depend on many factors, so we do not guarantee any specific number of calls, customers or sales.

11. If we make a mistake

If an error in your printed ad is our fault, such as a wrong phone number, we will make it right with a free spot in the next issue or a full refund for that issue, your choice. Our total responsibility for any issue is limited to the amount you paid for that issue.

12. Disputes

If anything goes wrong, please contact us first. We will work with you to fix it quickly. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

13. Privacy

We use your contact and business details only to create your ad, run the card and contact you about it. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number, and we do not sell your information.

14. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms for future issues. The version shown here on the date you pay applies to your purchase.
